# Content rules for Grand Theft Pundit

These rules are binding for every file in this directory: code, data, dialogue,
character art, mission text, radio ads, README copy. They are what makes the game
a parody in the GTA tradition instead of a hit piece. GTA never used a real
person or a real city: it built Liberty City, Vice City, Love Fist and Lazlow.
We do the same.

## 1. Every character is an original, fictional parody archetype

- Invent every name. Never use the name, nickname, handle, catchphrase, slogan,
  signature look, company, show title, or biographical details of any real
  person, living or dead. This includes politicians, heads of state,
  celebrities, musicians, journalists, influencers, crime victims, defendants,
  and private individuals who went viral.
- A name that is a thin edit of a real name (one letter changed, first name
  swapped, same surname) counts as a real name. Make names that are clearly
  invented and funny on their own.
- Archetypes are fair game. A campus-debate pundit with a folding table and a
  podcast. A real-estate-mogul president who names everything after himself.
  A music mogul who runs an empire of "parties". A hawkish foreign prime
  minister of an invented allied nation. A spook agency with a ridiculous
  name. A crypto evangelist. A think-tank donor. A wellness grifter.
  Satirize the role, the industry, and the incentives, never one real human.
- Visual designs are parametric and generic. No portrait may be a recognizable
  likeness of a real person.

## 2. No real events, deaths, crimes, or conspiracy theories

- No reference to any real assassination, shooting, death, murder, trial,
  indictment, arrest, scandal, or lawsuit, direct or coded.
- No conspiracy-theory narrative about any real event, and no plot in which a
  real country, ethnic group, religion, or agency secretly controls the world.
  An invented agency can run an absurd invented plot. A fictional ally nation
  can have a blowhard leader. Nothing may map onto an antisemitic, racist, or
  other hateful trope ("they control the media/banks/government" is banned in
  every costume).
- Invented countries get invented names that do not point at a real country.
  Invented agencies get invented names.

## 3. Punch at power and absurdity, not at protected groups

- No slurs. No jokes whose punchline is someone's race, ethnicity, religion,
  nationality, gender, sexuality, disability, or body.
- No sexual content, no nudity, no sexualized characters, and no jokes about
  sexual violence or trafficking.
- No content involving minors in any adult context.
- The protagonist is the butt of the joke as much as anyone. The target of the
  satire is the pundit-industrial complex: outrage economics, sponsor reads,
  donor money, think tanks, cable hits, ratio culture, and the spooks who
  profit from all of it.

## 4. Violence is cartoon arcade violence

- Top-down GTA 2 style: tiny sprites, pixel puffs, cars that bounce, "WASTED"
  screens. No gore, no execution scenes, no torture, no realistic weapons
  fetishism.
- No violence directed at any real person or recognizable stand-in.

## 5. Tone

- Smart, fast, absurd. Think a late-night sketch that happens to be a crime
  game. Dialogue is punchy, two or three lines at a time.
- Keep it PG-13: mild profanity is fine, nothing stronger.

## Enforcement

- Any agent or contributor who finds content that breaks these rules fixes it
  on the spot and reports it. "It's just a joke" is not a defense; these rules
  are the joke's rules.
- `tests/content_audit.js` scans all content for a banned-term list and must
  stay green.
